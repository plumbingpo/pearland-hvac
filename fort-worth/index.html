Getting More Years From Your HVAC System in Fort Worth

Many mid-season breakdowns trace back to skipped maintenance, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Swap your filter every one to three months, clear vegetation at least two feet from the outdoor condenser, and rinse the condenser coil once a year in spring. Then book a professional tune-up each spring and fall, one for cooling and one for heating, so our technicians can catch small problems while they're still cheap to fix.

How old is your system? Its age tells you a lot. With regular service, most air conditioners make it to 12 to 15 years, gas furnaces 15 to 20, and heat pumps 10 to 15. If your system is beyond that range and is also showing rising bills, rooms that never quite reach temperature, or frequent repairs, it's usually due for a planned replacement, which is almost always cheaper and less stressful than a rushed emergency install when the first heat wave hits.

If comfort problems do show up, acting quickly keeps them small. A weak capacitor is a quick fix. The same capacitor left alone for a season can take the compressor with it and turn a minor repair into a full system replacement. It's smart to keep our number saved on your phone so you can reach us at the first sign of trouble. Early intervention saves time, money, and miserable nights.
