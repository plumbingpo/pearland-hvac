Food Service Comfort

Restaurant and Food Service HVAC

A commercial kitchen is one of the hardest spaces to keep comfortable. Every burner and fryer adds heat, the exhaust hoods draw conditioned air out nonstop, and the makeup air unit has to replace that air without upsetting the building's pressure. Pearland HVAC services, sizes, and repairs the rooftop units, split systems, and makeup air equipment that keep the kitchen and dining room comfortable, and we plan service around your operating hours.

Air balance is where many restaurant HVAC problems start. When a kitchen runs negative, it pulls dining-room air the wrong way, drags hot or cold outdoor air through every door, and wears out your staff at peak service. We balance makeup air against hood exhaust and log every visit in written reports that landlords and auditors can review.

We work directly with owners, managers, and kitchen staff to plan replacements and maintenance that protect your business and fit your operating schedule. Our technicians know how to work in occupied facilities, installing during off-hours when needed, minimizing disruption, and keeping your kitchen running and your dining room comfortable.

Safe Installation

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

Commercial property owners have a duty of care to maintain safe, comfortable conditions for employees, customers, and tenants. A poorly installed or neglected HVAC system can result in carbon monoxide hazards, poor indoor air quality, refrigerant leaks, and costly water damage from clogged condensate lines. Pearland HVAC provides commercial installations and inspections that cover heat exchanger integrity, proper venting and combustion air, electrical connections and disconnects, refrigerant charge verification, condensate drainage protection, and ventilation rates for occupied spaces. Our written reports identify risks, assign priority levels, and recommend specific preventive actions.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and auditors. Building owners benefit from maintaining regular professional HVAC service and acting on expert recommendations. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature and filter records, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, gyms, and large mechanical rooms.

Emergency Service

Commercial Emergency HVAC Response in Pearland

Every hour without climate control costs your business money. In a restaurant, it can shut down the kitchen. On a retail floor, it sends shoppers home on a summer afternoon. For a server room, it risks overheated equipment, and in a leased building it invites tenant disputes. Pearland HVAC offers fast emergency HVAC service for commercial properties throughout Pearland and surrounding areas, dispatching an experienced technician quickly when you call.

Failed compressors, burned-out condenser fan motors, refrigerant leaks, ignition and gas valve faults, tripped high-limit switches, frozen coils, control board lockouts: our technicians have seen them all on commercial equipment. The priority is pinpointing the fault and getting heating or cooling back as quickly as possible. Once it's running, we set up maintenance and monitoring to prevent a repeat, and every response is fully documented for your records.

Scheduled HVAC Maintenance for Commercial Properties

A scheduled maintenance program from Pearland HVAC is built around your equipment, how the space is used, and your budget.
